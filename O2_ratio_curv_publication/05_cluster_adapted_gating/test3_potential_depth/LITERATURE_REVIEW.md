## Related Modified Gravity Theories (Comparison)

### 1. **MOND (Milgrom 1983)**

**Formula:** μ(a/a₀) · a = g_N  

**Key differences:**
- Uses acceleration threshold a₀ (not potential depth)
- No geometry dependence (no Σ̂, no ∇Σ)
- Interpolating function μ() between regimes
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both amplify effects at different scales
**Difference:** MOND uses acceleration, we use potential + geometry

---

### 2. **Vainshtein Screening (Vainshtein 1972, Babichev+ 2009)**

**Concept:** Modifications suppressed inside Vainshtein radius r_V

**Formula:** r_V ~ (r_g² · r)^(1/3) where r_g is gravitational radius

**Key differences:**
- Screening radius determines where modifications activate
- No explicit geometry (Σ, ∇Σ) dependence
- Designed to recover GR in Solar System
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both have scale-dependent effects
**Difference:** Vainshtein uses proximity to source, we use potential depth + geometry

---

### 3. **Chameleon Mechanism (Khoury & Weltman 2004)**

**Concept:** Scalar field mass depends on environment density

**Formula:** m_eff(ρ) = m₀ + β·ρ/M_Pl

**Key differences:**
- Density-dependent screening
- No geometric features (surface density gradients)
- No potential depth dependence
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both environment-dependent
**Difference:** Chameleon uses local density, we use geometry + potential

---

### 4. **f(R) Gravity (Sotiriou & Faraoni 2010)**

**Formula:** Action includes f(R) where R is Ricci scalar

**Key differences:**
- Curvature-dependent (spacetime curvature R)
- No surface density or gradient terms
- Field equations are 4th order
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both modify Einstein field equations
**Difference:** f(R) is spacetime curvature, we use projected surface density geometry

---

### 5. **DGP (Dvali-Gabadadze-Porrati 2000)**

**Concept:** Gravity leaks into extra dimension beyond crossover scale

**Formula:** Modified Friedmann equation with r_c (crossover scale)

**Key differences:**
- Extra dimensional
- No geometry gating
- Scale set by cosmological constant
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both scale-dependent
**Difference:** DGP is dimensional, we're 4D with geometry

---

### 6. **Emergent Gravity (Verlinde 2011)**

**Concept:** Gravity as entropic force

**Formula:** F = T · ∇S (entropic force)

**Key differences:**
- Holographic principle based
- Uses entanglement entropy
- No explicit Φ-dependence like ours
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both potentially emergent
**Difference:** Verlinde uses entropy, we use observable geometry + potential

---

### 7. **Scalar-Tensor Theories (Brans-Dicke 1961, Horndeski 2011)**

**General form:** G_μν + modifications = 8πG·φ²·T_μν

**Key differences:**
- Scalar field φ couples to matter
- Field has own dynamics (Klein-Gordon equation)
- No explicit geometry gating (Σ̂, ∇Σ)
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both have screening mechanisms (potential)
**Difference:** Scalar-tensor is field-theoretic, ours is phenomenological + geometric

---

### 8. **Weyl Gravity / Conformal Gravity (Mannheim & Kazanas 1989)**

**Concept:** Fourth-order conformal gravity

**Formula:** Action ~ ∫ C_μνρσ C^μνρσ √-g d⁴x

**Key differences:**
- Conformal invariance
- Fits rotation curves with γ·r term (linear potential)
- No surface density geometry
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both fit flat rotation curves
**Difference:** Weyl is fundamental theory, we're phenomenological geometry

---

### 9. **Superfluid Dark Matter (Berezhiani & Khoury 2015)**

**Concept:** Dark matter as phonons in superfluid

**Formula:** Effective gravity has MOND-like behavior in superfluid phase

**Key differences:**
- Requires dark matter (we don't assume DM)
- Phase transition mechanism
- No explicit geometry gating
- **Not the same as our approach**

**Similarity:** Both explain flat rotation curves
**Difference:** Superfluid DM still has dark matter, we modify gravity directly
