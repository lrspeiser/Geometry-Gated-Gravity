# Paper Strategy: Extension vs. New Paper

**Date:** October 2, 2025  
**Decision Point:** Should potential depth gating be O2 extension or standalone paper?

---

## Two Options

### Option A: **Extend O2 Paper** (Single unified paper)

**Pros:**
- Complete story (galaxies + clusters in one paper)
- Shows full model evolution (3-param → 4-param)
- Demonstrates broad applicability
- Single citation for future users
- Stronger impact (solves both scales)

**Cons:**
- Very long paper (~25-30 pages)
- Multiple complex topics
- Risk of reviewers rejecting entire paper if one part has issues
- Dilutes galaxy-only results (which are already strong)
- Harder to write concisely

---

### Option B: **Separate Papers** (Two sequential papers)

**Paper 1: O2 Galaxy Paper (already drafted)**
- Title: "Geometry-Gated Gravity: Flat Galaxy Rotation Curves from Surface Density and Curvature"
- Focus: SPARC galaxies only
- 3 parameters (a, b, d)
- Median APE 0.242
- Clean, focused story
- Submit immediately

**Paper 2: Potential Depth Extension (new)**
- Title: "Gravitational Potential Depth Gating: Extending Geometry-Gated Gravity to Cluster Scales"
- Focus: Cluster lensing (with galaxy validation)
- 4 parameters (a, b, d, β)
- Builds on Paper 1
- Submit after Paper 1 accepted

**Pros:**
- Each paper focused and clean
- Paper 1 publishable NOW (galaxies alone are strong)
- Paper 2 can take time to fit cluster data properly
- Lower risk (if cluster extension has issues, doesn't sink galaxy paper)
- Two publications > one publication

**Cons:**
- Need to wait for Paper 1 before submitting Paper 2 (citation)
- Readers need both papers for full picture
- Some repetition between papers (methods, etc.)

---

## Recommendation: **Option B - Separate Papers**

### Reasoning:

1. **Galaxy results are publication-ready NOW**
   - All figures done
   - Manuscript complete
   - Median APE 0.242 is excellent
   - Why delay this?

2. **Cluster extension needs more work**
   - Need to compute actual |Φ|(R) profiles
   - Need to fit β on real Einstein radii
   - Need to validate on SPARC (check APE < 0.30)
   - Could take 2-4 weeks

3. **Risk management**
   - If cluster extension has unforeseen issues, doesn't block galaxy paper
   - Reviewers may have concerns about cluster claims
   - Better to get galaxy paper accepted first, then tackle clusters

4. **Publication strategy**
   - Paper 1 (galaxies): ApJ or MNRAS (high-profile, focused)
   - Paper 2 (clusters): Follow-up in same journal
   - Shows research program, not one-off result

5. **Timing**
   - Paper 1 can submit THIS WEEK
   - Paper 2 can submit in ~1 month (after cluster fitting)
   - vs. Combined paper ready in ~1 month, but riskier
